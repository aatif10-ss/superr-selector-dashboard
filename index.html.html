How to interpret this dashboard

These are historical performance indicators, not predictions of auction availability

Pressure sample: balls or legal deliveries classified as high-pressure in the scoring model.
Baseline: performance outside pressure situations.
Batting rate: strike rate (runs per 100 balls). Higher is generally better.
Bowling rate: economy (runs per six legal balls). Lower is generally better.
Ranking score: model-specific composite. Batting and bowling scores are not directly comparable.
Important: historical performance does not establish IPL 2027 auction availability or guarantee future results.
Source: supplied SupeRR Selector CSV datasets and final Unstop ranking exports. Rankings and numbers are fixed to the previously prepared submission. This dashboard is a standalone offline HTML file; no external libraries or network access are required.
